What areas do you serve?

We serve homes and properties throughout Maryland and nearby areas. Give us a call to check if you are in our service area.

How often should my lawn be mowed?

Most lawns need weekly mowing from late spring through summer when grass grows fastest. When growth slows down in the fall, every other week usually works.

When is the best time to put down mulch?

Spring is the most popular time, right after cleanup. Mulch helps hold moisture and keep weeds down through the summer. A fall touch up also helps protect roots over the winter.

What comes with a spring or fall clean up?

We clear out leaves, sticks and dead growth, clean up the beds, trim back shrubs and haul the debris away so your yard is ready for the next season.

Do you offer snow plowing?

Yes. We plow snow during the winter. Spots fill up, so call before the first storm to get on the list.
